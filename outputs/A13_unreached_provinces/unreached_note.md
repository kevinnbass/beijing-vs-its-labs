# Shares for home-made computing: the sixteen governments an earlier note left open

*Of the sixteen province-level governments the project's first note on provincial targets left open, two set a share for home-made computing in a plan that was read: Guangdong, 70% of newly added computing by the end of 2025, as an aim; and Zhejiang, over 60% of the computing of one sector, its information and communications industry, by 2030, as the target of a project in that sector's plan. For twelve more a document was read in its own text and sets no share; in two of those a reward, not a target, goes to data centres whose basic hardware and software are 90% or more home-made: Shanxi's, in measures in force to the end of 2026, and Ningxia's, in a policy the region repealed by a decision signed Nov 27, 2025. Tibet's plan is known only from accounts of it, its issuer's own among them, and for Xinjiang no computing plan's text was found, though the regional planner says two were issued.*

## The question

The project's first note on provincial targets (Home-made chip targets by province), as first written on Nov 23, 2026, found seven province-level governments with a published share for home-made computing, each by its plan's own measure: chips, equipment, hardware and software, or computing capacity. One of the seven shares, Shandong's, is known only from the provincial daily's account of its plan. It did not reach eleven (Zhejiang, Chongqing, Anhui, Shaanxi, Shanxi, Jiangxi, Guangxi, Qinghai, Hainan, Xinjiang, Tibet). For five more (Guangdong, Jilin, Heilongjiang, Ningxia, Yunnan) the document it read was a plan for artificial intelligence or for the digital industry, not a computing plan. This note takes those sixteen. For each it asks one thing: does a plan of the government set a share of home-made chips, home-made computing or independently controllable computing?

## The answer in short

**1.** **Two governments set a share: Guangdong and Zhejiang.** Guangdong's computing plan for 2024 and 2025 says: "力争到2025年底，新增国产化算力占比达到70%" [1] (strive for home-made computing to make up 70% of newly added computing by the end of 2025). The page is on the site of the plan's lead issuing body. It is an aim, and the page does not mark it binding or indicative. Zhejiang's share was found on Nov 25, 2026, after this note was first written, and it is not in a computing plan. The plan for the province's information and communications industry for 2026 to 2030, posted on Jul 29, 2026 on the site of the Zhejiang Communications Administration, which issued it with the provincial development and reform commission, gives as the target of its fourth key project, on the computing network: "到2030年，浙江信息通信行业算力规模达150EFLOPS，国产化算力占比超60%" [2] (by 2030 the computing of Zhejiang's information and communications industry reaches 150 EFLOPS, with home-made computing over 60% of it). The share is for the computing of one sector, and it is a project's target: the plan's table of eighteen indicators, each marked indicative or binding, has no line for home-made computing. Zhejiang's AI measures of 2025, the document this note first read for it, appeal to buyers, "倡导政府部门、算力中心、经营主体采购或应用国产软硬件" [3] (advocate that government departments, computing centres and businesses buy or use home-made software and hardware), and set voucher rates and no share. The two are among the nine governments, of 31, found to set a share; the argument's table gives each in its document's own words. They do not count the same thing, so the numbers cannot be added or averaged.

**2.** **Two governments tied a reward, not a target, to data centres whose basic hardware and software are 90% or more home-made: Shanxi, whose measures are in force, and Ningxia, whose policy was repealed by a decision signed in November 2025.** Shanxi's measures of 2024, in force from Jul 1, 2024 to Dec 31, 2026, give up to 5 million yuan to a new or rebuilt data centre whose basic software and hardware are 90% or more home-made [4]. In Ningxia, the industry department's reply to a people's-congress suggestion, published May 12, 2025, gives the like reward of a regional policy, the opinions on policies for building the Ningxia hub of the national computing network: up to 10 million yuan for a data centre whose basic software and hardware are 90% or more home-made [5]. The regional government repealed those opinions, and the AI measures of 2023, both issued by its general office, by its decision 宁政规发〔2025〕5号, signed Nov 27, 2025 [6]. In the pages read, neither government sets a target; Shanxi's computing plan proper was not read, and Ningxia's hub plan is known only from a newspaper's account.

**3.** **Three governments the first note did not reach: in the copies read, none sets a share for home-made hardware.** Chongqing's plan of June 2026 for an energy and computing hub sets shares for two things only: green power, "确保新建数据中心绿电占比超过80%" [7] (make sure green power is over 80% in newly built data centres), and, in its table of indicators, the take-up of new smart terminals and agents, 80% by 2027; the word 国产 (home-made) is not in the page. Shaanxi's AI Plus plan of November 2025, held as a Sohu copy that lacks items 6 and 7 and in two full copies on other sites, has its computing item whole: "按需建设算力基础设施" [8] (build computing infrastructure as needed). Hainan's AI Plus plan of 2026, held as a copy on a policy site, says "构建自主可控算力底座" [9] (build an independently controllable computing base) and gives no number; the copy marks the published text as abridged, so a clause left out of it cannot be excluded.

**4.** **Five more governments: a document was read in its own text and sets no share.** Qinghai's two plans, of 2024 and 2026, and Jiangxi's two documents are about computing. Qinghai's 2026 plan tells computing centres to deploy independently controllable, secure and reliable equipment [10]; Jiangxi's measures of December 2025 say the share of independently controllable equipment is to rise step by step, with no number [11]. Anhui's, Jilin's and Guangxi's are wider plans, for digital infrastructure or the digital economy. With point 3 that makes eight governments in this group (Anhui, Jilin, Guangxi, Jiangxi, Qinghai, Chongqing, Shaanxi, Hainan).

**5.** **Two governments: no computing plan was found, and the first note's document stands** (Heilongjiang, Yunnan). Each is a plan for AI or for the digital industry and sets no share.

**6.** **Tibet: accounts only.** The region's computing plan for 2024 to 2026 is known from two accounts of it: the Tibet Communications Administration's own news item on its site, Jun 25, 2024 [12], and a trade newspaper's account [13]. Both give its targets for 2026, "到2026年，算力规模达到100PFLOPS，智算算力占比达到60%" (computing reaches 100 PFLOPS, and intelligent computing 60% of it), and neither has any of the home-made words or the word 芯片 (chip). An account that is silent does not show that the plan is silent.

**7.** **Xinjiang: no computing plan's text was found.** Four pages on the region's own sites were read instead. The two of them cited here, the region's plan for economic and social development for 2024 [14] and the regional planner's reply on computing posted Sept 5, 2025, were read and scanned by script: they set no share for home-made hardware, and the one use of independently controllable in them, in the reply, is about a network protocol [15]. Read later, on Nov 25, 2026: in another reply, posted Jul 24, 2026, the regional planner says two computing plans were issued, and their texts were not reached [16]; and the General Office's measures of 2026 for profit-oriented services, published abridged, set a target for intelligent computing and a subsidy for its users, with none of the words 国产 (home-made), 自主可控 or 芯片 (chip) [17].

**8.** **All 31.** With the first note's governments, the project's table of all 31 provincial-level governments (tables/provinces_31.tsv) now has nine that set a share, 20 with a plan read that sets none, one known only from accounts (Tibet) and one with no computing plan found (Xinjiang), as the project's argument (point 3) gives them. Each row of this note agrees with that table.

**9.** **Two more documents, outside the sixteen, were read: Gansu's AI plan and Hunan's plan for green intelligent computing.** Neither sets a share. Hunan's plan has the figure 95%, but it is the present rate of home-made equipment for making chips, not a goal for computing [18]. They are in table 2.

## Method

- **When.** The note was first written on the morning of Nov 23, 2026, from 84 requests: 44 web searches, 34 page saves and 6 calls to the Internet Archive (four asking whether it holds a page, two fetching the captures it held), each listed, numbered, in request_log.txt. Every later change rests on pages the project saved and read after that, with no further request for this note: provincial pages and Internet Archive captures saved later on Nov 23, 2026; on Nov 24, 2026, the Tibet administration's own news item; on Nov 25, 2026, Zhejiang's sector plan and its government's signed PDF of the AI measures, Ningxia's decision of repeal, the two full copies of Shaanxi's plan, the saved capture of Jiangxi's measures, and the Xinjiang planner's reply and services measures.
- **What each row rests on.** **Saved official page**: a copy in the project's store of a page on the issuing body's own site or its government's own site. **Saved official PDF**: the same for a PDF on a government site. **Saved Archive capture of the official page**: a copy in the project's store of an Internet Archive capture of such a page. **Saved Archive captures: one of the official page, one of a copy on a city government's site**: Jiangxi's row, whose industry plan rests on a capture of the department's own page and whose measures rest on a capture of the Jingdezhen city government's copy. **Saved copy on another site**: a copy in the project's store of the document's own text on any other site. **A press account saved from another site**: a saved page that describes the plan; the plan's own text is not held.
- **Units.** PFLOPS and EFLOPS measure computing capacity: a PFLOPS is a million billion calculations (floating-point operations) a second, and an EFLOPS a thousand PFLOPS (our gloss). A share of intelligent computing, as in Tibet's and Guangxi's targets, is that kind of computing's part of the whole capacity, not a share of home-made hardware.
- **No target share for home-made hardware in the page read: the check and its control.** A script listed, in 31 pages cited here (all but the first note's own pages, which that note checked, a research firm's report, and the four PDFs, read page by page as images), each place where a home-made word (国产, 自主可控, 安全可控, 安全可靠, 自主化, 信创) stands within 60 characters of a percent sign: seven places in six pages, listed in near_percent.txt. Each was read. Three show that the scan can see a share: Guangdong's 70%, Zhejiang's 60% and Shanxi's reward at 90%. The other four are not target shares for home-made hardware: a goal for intelligent computing (50%) beside the words for an independently controllable ecosystem, in Guangdong's plan; a rate for public data (80%) beside a heading on secure and reliable data infrastructure, in Guangxi's; a rate of clean-energy use (100%) beside the words secure and reliable, in Qinghai's 2024 plan; and Hunan's 95%, the present rate of home-made equipment for making chips, not a goal. In four documents, Chongqing's hub plan, Zhejiang's AI measures, Shaanxi's AI plan and Hainan's AI plan, every percentage in the page was read as well: Chongqing's are for green power and the take-up of new smart terminals and agents; those of Zhejiang's AI measures are rates and thresholds of vouchers, subsidies and loan support; Shaanxi's is the take-up of new smart terminals and agents; the copy of Hainan's plan has none outside the copying site's own advertising.
- **A word is not in a page: the check and its control.** Where a row says a word is not in a page, the script counted it at zero and found a control word in the same page, so the count could have seen a hit: 19 such checks, listed in absent_check.txt.
- **No plan was found: what that rests on.** It is a statement about searches, not about the government. The searches and their controls are in the section after the tables.
- **PDFs read as images.** Every page of the four saved PDFs (Qinghai's two plans, Hunan's plan and Gansu's) was opened and read as an image. Gansu's has a sound text layer. For Qinghai's two and Hunan's, the saved captures of the same pages have sound text, and the script compared it with what was typed from the images: of 56 typed lines, 45 stand word for word in the captures (five of them table rows, once a category cell that the typing repeats is set aside). The other 11 are the table and one box of Qinghai's 2026 plan, which are images in the capture too and so rest on the page image alone. The comparison is listed in transcription_check.txt.
- **Dates.** Each date is the one the page gives, and the page's own date string was checked by the script.

## What a reader may and may not conclude

- **May.** That Guangdong has published a share: 70% of newly added computing by the end of 2025, as an aim. That Zhejiang's plan for its information and communications industry gives, as a project's target, home-made computing over 60% of that sector's computing by 2030. That nine of the 31 governments were found to set a share. That Shanxi's measures, in force to the end of 2026, reward data centres whose basic hardware and software are 90% or more home-made, and that Ningxia's like reward stood in a policy the region repealed by a decision signed Nov 27, 2025; in the pages read, neither sets a target. That the documents read for Chongqing, Shaanxi and Hainan set no share for home-made hardware; Chongqing's plan sets shares only for green power and for the take-up of new smart terminals and agents.
- **May not.** That the governments without a share here have none anywhere: for Shaanxi, Hainan, Guangxi, Jilin, Anhui, Heilongjiang and Yunnan the document read is not a computing plan proper, Shanxi's and Chongqing's earlier computing plans were not read, Ningxia's hub plan and Tibet's plan are known from accounts, and for Xinjiang no plan was found. That Guangdong's aim was met: no page read says. That the aim binds a private company's purchases: the page sets a goal for the province's newly added computing. That Zhejiang's share is for the province's computing as a whole, or is one of its plan's indicators: it is a project's target for one sector, and the plan's table of indicators gives intelligent computing with no home-made line. That Zhejiang's is a computing plan of the provincial government: it is a sector plan of the communications administration and the planning commission. That any of this is a national target: none is reported here.
