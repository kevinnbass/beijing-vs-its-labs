# Shares for home-made computing: the sixteen governments an earlier note left open

@deck Of the sixteen province-level governments the project's first note on provincial targets left open, two set a share for home-made computing in a plan that was read: Guangdong, 70% of newly added computing by the end of 2025, as an aim; and Zhejiang, over 60% of the computing of one sector, its information and communications industry, by 2030, as the target of a project in that sector's plan. For {{N_READ}} more a document was read in its own text and sets no share; two of those governments tied a reward, not a target, to data centres whose basic hardware and software are 90% or more home-made: Shanxi, in measures in force to the end of 2026, and Ningxia, by its industry department's account of a regional policy that the region repealed by a decision signed Nov 27, 2025. Tibet's plan is known only from accounts of it, its issuer's own among them, and for Xinjiang no computing plan's text was found, though the regional planner says two were issued.

## The question

The project's first note on provincial targets (Home-made chip targets by province), as first written on Nov 23, 2026, found seven province-level governments with a published share for home-made computing, each by its plan's own measure: chips, equipment, hardware and software, or computing capacity. One of the seven shares, Shandong's, is known only from press accounts of its plan. It did not reach eleven (Zhejiang, Chongqing, Anhui, Shaanxi, Shanxi, Jiangxi, Guangxi, Qinghai, Hainan, Xinjiang, Tibet). For five more (Guangdong, Jilin, Heilongjiang, Ningxia, Yunnan) the document it read was a plan for artificial intelligence or for the digital industry, not a computing plan. This note takes those sixteen. For each it asks one thing: does a plan of the government set a share of home-made chips, home-made computing or independently controllable computing?

## The answer in short

1. **Two governments set a share: Guangdong and Zhejiang.** Guangdong's computing plan for 2024 and 2025 says: "力争到2025年底，新增国产化算力占比达到70%" [cn-o39b-guangdong-yuesuan-2024] (strive for home-made computing to make up 70% of newly added computing by the end of 2025). The page is on the site of the plan's lead issuing body. It is an aim, and the page does not mark it binding or indicative. Zhejiang's share was found on Nov 25, 2026, after this note was first written, and it is not in a computing plan. The plan for the province's information and communications industry for 2026 to 2030, posted on Jul 29, 2026 on the site of the Zhejiang Communications Administration, which issued it with the provincial development and reform commission, gives as the target of its fourth key project, on the computing network: "到2030年，浙江信息通信行业算力规模达150EFLOPS，国产化算力占比超60%" [cn-ga31-cndocs-52] (by 2030 the computing of Zhejiang's information and communications industry reaches 150 EFLOPS, with home-made computing over 60% of it). The share is for the computing of one sector, and it is a project's target: the plan's table of eighteen indicators, each marked indicative or binding, has no line for home-made computing. Zhejiang's AI measures of 2025, the document this note first read for it, appeal to buyers, "倡导政府部门、算力中心、经营主体采购或应用国产软硬件" [cn-le25-zhejiang-ai-measures-2025-smartcity] (advocate that government departments, computing centres and businesses buy or use home-made software and hardware), and set voucher rates and no share. The two are among the {{N31_share}} governments, of 31, found to set a share; the argument's table gives each in its document's own words. They do not count the same thing, so the numbers cannot be added or averaged.

## Method

- **When.** The note was first written on the morning of Nov 23, 2026, from {{N_REQ}} requests: {{N_SEARCH}} web searches, {{N_SAVE}} page saves and {{N_ARCH}} calls to the Internet Archive ({{N_ARCH_ASK}} asking whether it holds a page, {{N_ARCH_GET}} fetching the captures it held), each listed, numbered, in request_log.txt. Every later change rests on pages the project saved and read after that, not on new searches or saves made for this note: provincial pages and Internet Archive captures saved later on Nov 23, 2026; on Nov 24, 2026, the Tibet administration's own news item; on Nov 25, 2026, Zhejiang's sector plan and its government's signed PDF of the AI measures, Ningxia's decision of repeal, the two full copies of Shaanxi's plan, the saved capture of Jiangxi's measures, the Xinjiang planner's reply and services measures, and a copy of Hainan's plan on the site of the Haikou Jiangdong New Area.
- **What each row rests on.** **Saved official page**: a copy in the project's store of a page on the issuing body's own site or its government's own site. **Saved official PDF**: the same for a PDF on a government site. **Saved Archive capture of the official page**: a copy in the project's store of an Internet Archive capture of such a page. **Saved Archive captures: one of the official page, one of a copy on a city government's site**: Jiangxi's row, whose industry plan rests on a capture of the department's own page and whose measures rest on a capture of the Jingdezhen city government's copy. **Saved copy on another site**: a copy in the project's store of the document's own text on any other site. **A press account saved from another site**: a saved page that describes the plan; the plan's own text is not held.
- **Units.** PFLOPS and EFLOPS measure computing capacity: a PFLOPS is a million billion calculations (floating-point operations) a second, and an EFLOPS a thousand PFLOPS (our gloss). A share of intelligent computing, as in Tibet's and Guangxi's targets, is that kind of computing's part of the whole capacity, not a share of home-made hardware.
- **No target share for home-made hardware in the page read: the check and its control.** A script listed, in {{N_SCAN}} pages cited here (all but the first note's own pages, which that note checked, a research firm's report, and the four PDFs, read page by page as images), each place where a home-made word (国产, 自主可控, 安全可控, 安全可靠, 自主化, 信创) stands within 60 characters of a percent sign: {{N_NEAR}} places in {{N_NEAR_PAGES}} pages, listed in near_percent.txt. Each was read. {{N_NEAR_SEEN_cap}} show that the scan can see a share: Guangdong's 70%, Zhejiang's 60% and Shanxi's reward at 90%. The other four are not target shares for home-made hardware: a goal for intelligent computing (50%) beside the words for an independently controllable ecosystem, in Guangdong's plan; a rate for public data (80%) beside a heading on secure and reliable data infrastructure, in Guangxi's; a rate of clean-energy use (100%) beside the words secure and reliable, in Qinghai's 2024 plan; and Hunan's 95%, the present rate of home-made equipment for making chips, not a goal. In four documents, Chongqing's hub plan, Zhejiang's AI measures, Shaanxi's AI plan and Hainan's AI plan, every percentage in the page was read as well: Chongqing's are for green power and the take-up of new smart terminals and agents; those of Zhejiang's AI measures are rates and thresholds of vouchers, subsidies and loan support; Shaanxi's is the take-up of new smart terminals and agents; neither copy of Hainan's plan has one in the plan's text: the policy site's one is in its own advertising, and those on the New Area's site stand in a script of the page.
- **A word is not in a page: the check and its control.** Where a row says a word is not in a page, the script counted it at zero and found a control word in the same page, so the count could have seen a hit: {{N_ABSENT}} such checks, listed in absent_check.txt.
- **No plan was found: what that rests on.** It is a statement about searches, not about the government. The searches and their controls are in the section after the tables.
- **PDFs read as images.** Every page of the four saved PDFs (Qinghai's two plans, Hunan's plan and Gansu's) was opened and read as an image. Gansu's has a sound text layer. For Qinghai's two and Hunan's, the saved captures of the same pages have sound text, and the script compared it with what was typed from the images: of {{TR_TOT}} typed lines, {{TR_SAME}} stand word for word in the captures ({{TR_CELL}} of them table rows, once a category cell that the typing repeats is set aside). The other {{TR_IMAGE}} are the table and one box of Qinghai's 2026 plan, which are images in the capture too and so rest on the page image alone. The comparison is listed in transcription_check.txt.
- **Dates.** Each date is the one the page gives, and the page's own date string was checked by the script.

## What a reader may and may not conclude

- **May.** That Guangdong has published a share: 70% of newly added computing by the end of 2025, as an aim. That Zhejiang's plan for its information and communications industry gives, as a project's target, home-made computing over 60% of that sector's computing by 2030. That {{N31_share}} of the 31 governments were found to set a share. That Shanxi's measures, in force to the end of 2026, reward data centres whose basic hardware and software are 90% or more home-made, and that Ningxia's like reward stood in a policy the region repealed by a decision signed Nov 27, 2025; in the pages read, neither sets a target. That the documents read for Chongqing, Shaanxi and Hainan set no share for home-made hardware; Chongqing's plan sets shares only for green power and for the take-up of new smart terminals and agents.
- **May not.** That the governments without a share here have none anywhere: for Shaanxi, Hainan, Guangxi, Jilin, Anhui, Heilongjiang and Yunnan the document read is not a computing plan proper, Shanxi's and Chongqing's earlier computing plans were not read, Ningxia's hub plan and Tibet's plan are known from accounts, and for Xinjiang no plan's text was found. That Guangdong's aim was met: no page read says. That the aim binds a private company's purchases: the page sets a goal for the province's newly added computing. That Zhejiang's share is for the province's computing as a whole, or is one of its plan's indicators: it is a project's target for one sector, and the plan's table of indicators gives intelligent computing with no home-made line. That Zhejiang's is a computing plan of the provincial government: it is a sector plan of the communications administration and the planning commission. That any of this is a national target: none is reported here.
