# Home-made chip shares: the sixteen governments left open

@deck Of the sixteen province-level governments the first note left open, two set a share for home-made computing in a plan that was read: Guangdong, 70% of newly added computing by the end of 2025, as an aim; and Zhejiang, over 60% of the computing of one sector, its information and communications industry, by 2030, as the target of a project in that sector's plan. For {{N_READ}} more a document was read in its own text and sets no share; two of those pay a reward at 90% or more. Tibet's plan is known only from accounts of it, its issuer's own among them, and for Xinjiang no computing plan was found.

## The question

The first note on idea 39 found seven province-level governments with a published share for home-made computing hardware. It did not reach eleven (Zhejiang, Chongqing, Anhui, Shaanxi, Shanxi, Jiangxi, Guangxi, Qinghai, Hainan, Xinjiang, Tibet). For five more (Guangdong, Jilin, Heilongjiang, Ningxia, Yunnan) the document it read was a plan for artificial intelligence or for the digital industry, not a computing plan. This note takes those sixteen. For each it asks one thing: does a plan of the government set a share of home-made chips, home-made computing or independently controllable computing?

## The answer in short

1. **Two governments set a share: Guangdong and Zhejiang.** Guangdong's computing plan for 2024 and 2025 says: "力争到2025年底，新增国产化算力占比达到70%" [cn-o39b-guangdong-yuesuan-2024] (strive for home-made computing to make up 70% of newly added computing by the end of 2025). The page is on the site of the plan's lead issuing body. It is an aim, and the page does not mark it binding or indicative. Zhejiang's share was found on Nov 25, 2026, after this note was first written, and it is not in a computing plan. The plan for the province's information and communications industry for 2026 to 2030, posted on Jul 29, 2026 on the site of the Zhejiang Communications Administration, which issued it with the provincial development and reform commission, gives as the target of its fourth key project, on the computing network: "到2030年，浙江信息通信行业算力规模达150EFLOPS，国产化算力占比超60%" [cn-ga31-cndocs-52] (by 2030 the computing of Zhejiang's information and communications industry reaches 150 EFLOPS, with home-made computing over 60% of it). The share is for the computing of one sector, and it is a project's target: the plan's table of eighteen indicators, each marked indicative or binding, has no line for home-made computing. Zhejiang's AI measures of 2025, the document this note first read for it, appeal to buyers, "倡导政府部门、算力中心、经营主体采购或应用国产软硬件" [cn-le25-zhejiang-ai-measures-2025-smartcity] (advocate that government departments, computing centres and businesses buy or use home-made software and hardware), and set voucher rates and no share. The two are among the {{N31_share}} governments, of 31, found to set a share; the argument's table gives each in its document's own words. They do not count the same thing, so the numbers cannot be added or averaged.

## Method

- **Two rounds.** Round 1, on the morning of Nov 23, 2026, made {{N_REQ}} requests: {{N_SEARCH}} web searches, {{N_SAVE}} page saves and {{N_ARCH}} calls to the Internet Archive through the machine's one queue, each listed in request_log.txt. Round 4, the same day, made none. It rests on pages saved since by two later agents (L16 and L25, whose ids begin cn-le16- and cn-le25-) and on Internet Archive captures the session filed (ids ending -wayback). Zhejiang's row was rewritten on Nov 25, 2026, with no web request by this note: it rests on a page that a later search of the project found and saved that day (its id begins cn-ga31-). Later that day the rows of Ningxia, Jiangxi, Shaanxi, Hainan and Xinjiang took in pages the project had saved and read (ids beginning cn-ga31-), again with no web request by this note.
- **What each row rests on.** **Saved official page**: a copy in the project's store of a page on the issuing body's own site or its government's own site. **Saved Archive capture of the official page**: a copy in the project's store of an Internet Archive capture of such a page. **Saved copy on another site**: a copy in the project's store of the document's own text on any other site. **A press account saved from another site**: a saved page that describes the plan; the plan's own text is not held.
- **No share in the page read: the check and its control.** A script listed, in the {{N_SCAN}} pages read in this pass or saved since, each place where a home-made word (国产, 自主可控, 安全可控, 安全可靠, 自主化, 信创) stands within 60 characters of a percent sign: {{N_NEAR}} places in {{N_NEAR_PAGES}} pages, listed in near_percent.txt. Each was read. {{N_NEAR_SEEN_cap}} show that the scan can see a share: Guangdong's 70%, Zhejiang's 60% and Shanxi's reward at 90%. The other four are not shares of home-made hardware: a goal for intelligent computing (50%) beside the words for an independently controllable ecosystem, in Guangdong's plan; a rate for public data (80%) beside a heading on secure and reliable data infrastructure, in Guangxi's; a rate of clean-energy use (100%) beside the words secure and reliable, in Qinghai's 2024 plan; and Hunan's 95% for chip-making equipment. For the four documents newly read in round 4, every percentage in the page was read as well: Chongqing's are for green power and the take-up of intelligent terminals; those of Zhejiang's AI measures are rates and thresholds of vouchers, subsidies and loan support; Shaanxi's is the take-up of intelligent terminals; the copy of Hainan's plan has none outside the copying site's own advertising. The scan was not run on the three damaged PDFs, which were read page by page as images, nor on the first note's pages, which that note checked.
- **A word is not in a page: the check and its control.** Where a row says a word is not in a page, the script counted it at zero and found a control word in the same page, so the count could have seen a hit: {{N_ABSENT}} such checks, listed in absent_check.txt.
- **No plan was found: what that rests on.** It is a statement about searches, not about the government. The searches and their controls are in the section after the tables.
- **Hand-saved PDFs.** Every page of the four PDFs the owner saved was opened and read as an image. Gansu's has a sound text layer. For Qinghai's two and Hunan's, the saved captures of the same pages have sound text, and the script compared it with what was typed from the images: of {{TR_TOT}} typed lines, {{TR_SAME}} stand word for word in the captures ({{TR_CELL}} of them table rows, once a category cell that the typing repeats is set aside). The other {{TR_IMAGE}} are the table and one box of Qinghai's 2026 plan, which are images in the capture too and so rest on the page image alone. The comparison is listed in transcription_check.txt.
- **Dates.** Each date is the one the page gives, and the page's own date string was checked by the script.

## What a reader may and may not conclude

- **May.** That Guangdong has published a share: 70% of newly added computing by the end of 2025, as an aim. That Zhejiang's plan for its information and communications industry gives, as a project's target, home-made computing over 60% of that sector's computing by 2030. That {{N31_share}} of the 31 governments were found to set a share. That Shanxi and Ningxia tie money to a rate of 90% or more and set no target in the pages read. That the documents read for Chongqing, Shaanxi and Hainan set no share for home-made hardware, Chongqing's setting one only for green power.
- **May not.** That the governments without a share here have none anywhere: for Shaanxi, Hainan, Guangxi, Jilin, Anhui, Heilongjiang and Yunnan the document read is not a computing plan proper, Shanxi's and Chongqing's earlier computing plans were not read, Ningxia's hub plan and Tibet's plan are known from accounts, and for Xinjiang no plan was found. That Guangdong's aim was met: no page read says. That the aim binds a private company's purchases: the page sets a goal for the province's newly added computing. That Zhejiang's share is for the province's computing as a whole, or is one of its plan's indicators: it is a project's target for one sector, and the plan's table of indicators gives intelligent computing with no home-made line. That Zhejiang's is a computing plan of the provincial government: it is a sector plan of the communications administration and the planning commission. That any of this is a national target: none is reported here.
