For Micron, Beijing published a conclusion: operators of critical information infrastructure shall stop buying. For Nvidia, a case and a summons.

What Beijing put in writing in two cases. In 2023 the cyberspace regulator’s review office published its conclusion that Micron’s products failed a security review, and that operators of critical information infrastructure shall stop buying them. From 2024 to 2026 the published acts found against Nvidia were an antitrust case and a summons, and state media warned about its chips in public. Instructions not to buy Nvidia chips were found only in press reports citing unnamed people; what companies told the US securities regulator speaks of a request to halt purchases of “certain chips”, naming no maker (Alibaba), and of restrictions on buying Nvidia’s products (Nvidia).

Read with care

  • Two findings hold together. Beijing can publish such a text, and for Nvidia none was found. Press reports citing unnamed people describe instructions of which no published text was found, and company filings speak of a request to halt purchases of “certain chips” and of restrictions. What the missing text means, the figure cannot say.
  • None found is a statement about what was read: the 82 policy pages read for this project. It is not a claim that no such text exists.
  • The Micron conclusion came from the Cybersecurity Review Office, which sits in the cyberspace regulator: “网络安全审查办公室设在国家互联网信息办公室” (the office is set up in the Cyberspace Administration). It speaks to operators of critical information infrastructure, not to every buyer, and it is not about Nvidia.
  • Alibaba’s filing names no chip maker: it says “certain chips”. Nvidia’s own reports changed their words over time: from Nov 19, 2025 the Chinese government “restricted customers from purchasing, importing, or using our data center products”; from Feb 25, 2026, “discouraged customers from purchasing, importing, or using our data center products”. From November 2025 the same reports also say Nvidia is “already effectively foreclosed from the China market by U.S. export controls”.
  • Open marks rest on unnamed people. In the answers found, Beijing’s spokespeople confirmed none of them: “我不了解你提到的情况” (I am not aware of what you mention), said the foreign ministry on Aug 13, 2025; asked about another report on Dec 10, 2025, it said “具体情况建议向中方主管部门询问” (for the details, ask the competent Chinese departments).
  • The marks under Nvidia are a selection, not a count of everything reported. English renderings of Chinese are ours, not official translations.
