1 of 12The declared line.

Beijing’s declared line is to use home-made chips. Xi Jinping told the Politburo to concentrate forces on high-end chips 1. The state planner (NDRC) said it was guiding home-made large models to step up their adaptation to home-made chips 2.

  • On the record: Xinhua’s account of the Politburo session of Apr 25, 2025 1; the state planner’s press conference of May 22, 2026 2
2 of 12What the companies used and ordered.

The companies kept using Nvidia chips and also trained on home-made chips. Baidu’s Qianfan-VL paper says “All models are trained entirely on Baidu’s Kunlun P800 chips” 3. A Shanghai government body wrote that in the city the clusters internet companies build themselves run mainly on Nvidia cards 4. Reported: orders “for more than 2 million H200 chips for 2026” 5.

  • On the record: Shanghai economy commission, reply of Apr 30, 2025 (published Nov 10) 4; Qianfan-VL paper, September 2025 3
  • Reported, from unnamed people: Reuters, Dec 31, 2025 5
3 of 12What Beijing did, on the record.

Regulators acted against Nvidia by name: an antitrust case 6 and a summons 7. Nvidia’s own filing says licensed H200 sales “were restricted by the PRC government” 8.

  • On the record: the market regulator, Dec 9, 2024 6; the cyberspace regulator, Jul 31, 2025 7; Nvidia, Form 10-Q for the quarter to Jul 26, 2026 8
4 of 12What Beijing did, as reported.

Reported, from unnamed people, and not published policy. The Wall Street Journal: “Authorities privately told companies to halt their Nvidia purchases” 9. A vice premier’s warning, in its words: “Anyone who resisted employing domestic chips was a traitor.” 9

  • Reported, from unnamed people: Wall Street Journal, Jul 23, 2026, looking back to the summer before; it gives no date for the warning; the vice premier is Ding Xuexiang 9
