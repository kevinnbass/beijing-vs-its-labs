1 of 12The declared line.

Beijing’s declared line is to use home-made chips. Xi Jinping told the Politburo to concentrate forces on high-end chips 1. The state planner (NDRC) said it was guiding home-made large models to step up their adaptation to home-made chips 2.

  • On the record: Xinhua’s account of the Politburo session of Apr 25, 2025 1; the state planner’s press conference of May 22, 2026 2
2 of 12What the companies used and ordered.

The companies went on using and ordering Nvidia chips. A Shanghai government body wrote that in the city internet companies’ own clusters run mainly on Nvidia cards 3. Reported: orders “for more than 2 million H200 chips for 2026” 4.

  • On the record: Shanghai Commission of Economy and Informatization, written reply dated Apr 30, 2025 (published Nov 10, 2025) 3
  • Reported, from unnamed people: Reuters, Dec 31, 2025 4
3 of 12What Beijing did, on the record.

Regulators acted against Nvidia by name: an antitrust case 5 and a summons 6. Nvidia’s own filing says licensed H200 sales “were restricted by the PRC government” 7.

  • On the record: the market regulator, Dec 9, 2024 5; the cyberspace regulator, Jul 31, 2025 6; Nvidia, Form 10-Q for the quarter to Jul 26, 2026 7
4 of 12What Beijing did, as reported.

Reported, from unnamed people, and not published policy. The Wall Street Journal: “Authorities privately told companies to halt their Nvidia purchases” 8. A vice premier’s warning, in its words: “Anyone who resisted employing domestic chips was a traitor.” 8

  • Reported, from unnamed people: Wall Street Journal, Jul 23, 2026, looking back to the summer before; it gives no date for the warning; the vice premier is Ding Xuexiang 8

What Beijing meant by it is not settled. A US commission saw in the antitrust move against Nvidia “China’s willingness to both exert leverage and ease pressure as a negotiating tactic” 26.
