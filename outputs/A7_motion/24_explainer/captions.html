The captions

1The declared line. Beijing's declared line is to use home-made chips. Xi Jinping told the Politburo to concentrate forces on high-end chips 1. The state planner (NDRC) said it was guiding home-made large models to step up their adaptation to home-made chips 2.

  • On the record: Xinhua's account of the Politburo session of Apr 25, 2025 1; the state planner's press conference of May 22, 2026 2

2What the companies used and ordered. The companies kept using Nvidia chips and also trained on home-made chips. Baidu’s Qianfan-VL paper says “All models are trained entirely on Baidu’s Kunlun P800 chips” 3. A Shanghai government body wrote that in the city the clusters internet companies build themselves run mainly on Nvidia cards 4. Reported: orders “for more than 2 million H200 chips for 2026” 5.

  • On the record: Shanghai economy commission, reply of Apr 30, 2025 (published Nov 10) 4; Qianfan-VL paper, September 2025 3
  • Reported, from unnamed people: Reuters, Dec 31, 2025 5

3What Beijing did, on the record. Regulators acted against Nvidia by name: an antitrust case 6 and a summons 7. Nvidia's own filing says licensed H200 sales “were restricted by the PRC government” 8.

  • On the record: the market regulator, Dec 9, 2024 6; the cyberspace regulator, Jul 31, 2025 7; Nvidia, Form 10-Q for the quarter to Jul 26, 2026 8

4What Beijing did, as reported. Reported, from unnamed people, and not published policy. The Wall Street Journal: “Authorities privately told companies to halt their Nvidia purchases” 9. A vice premier's warning, in its words: “Anyone who resisted employing domestic chips was a traitor.” 9

  • Reported, from unnamed people: Wall Street Journal, Jul 23, 2026, looking back to the summer before; it gives no date for the warning; the vice premier is Ding Xuexiang 9

5The numbers. Nvidia's H200 chip in numbers: four separate counts at four dates, not one tally. Washington's is from a government table; the other three are press reports citing unnamed people.

  • Reported: ordered by Chinese companies for 2026: “more than 2 million H200 chips”. Reuters, Dec 31, 2025 5
  • Licensed by Washington for Nvidia: 579,868 chips, each application “Approved, not shipped”. Licence table released by a House committee, status at May 11, 2026; our sum 10
  • Reported: approved by Beijing, with conditions: “more than 400,000 H200 chips in total”. Reuters, Jan 28, 2026 11
  • Reported: stored in Hong Kong, November 2026: “About 600,000”. Reuters, Nov 5, 2026 12

6Limit 1 of 5. Beijing was not one actor. Some local governments and units of the telecom carriers were also more open to Nvidia. The commerce minister told Nvidia's chief executive he hoped multinationals, Nvidia among them, would give Chinese customers high-quality, reliable products and services 13. Reported: the commerce ministry “favoured imports” 14.

  • On the record: Ministry of Commerce, account of the meeting of Jul 17, 2025 13
  • Reported, from unnamed people: Financial Times, Nov 13, 2026 14
  • From the project's referenced argument, points 5 and 17: the evidence on local governments and carriers.

Of 297 state and Party media items on Nvidia and its chips found from July 2025 to November 2026, 34 only warn (33 of them about the chips for AI computing; one, a report of a forum, about home-made software and hardware at large), and 23 of the 34 date from Jul 31 to Sept 24, 2025, and 6 from Dec 9, 2025 to Jan 14, 2026 in reports on the American easing for the H200.

8Limit 3 of 5. Third, Nvidia's own filings report licensed sales to China, and Reuters reported, citing people it did not name, that some purchases were approved (point 19). A US official told Congress: “There have been minimal exports of any H200s to China so far” 31.

9Limit 4 of 5. The companies had reasons of their own. In the records the project holds, American curbs and conditions are named as causes far more often than Chinese acts. Alibaba says its chip unit “has brought proprietary GPUs into scaled production” 34. Nvidia: “we are already effectively foreclosed from the China market by U.S. export controls” 35. Washington returned without action the applications for 55% of the chips its summary of May 11, 2026 records; its table gives no reason 10.

  • From the project's referenced argument, points 11 and 20: the comparison of the causes named in the records.

10Limit 5 of 5. What Beijing meant by it is not settled. A US commission saw in the antitrust move against Nvidia “China's willingness to both exert leverage and ease pressure as a negotiating tactic” 37. Asked whether China would let its companies buy the H200, the US Trade Representative called it “a sovereign decision for China” 38. The direction stands whether Beijing meant its acts as lasting policy, as leverage in trade talks or as protection of its home market (point 21).

  • On the record: US-China Economic and Security Review Commission, 2025 annual report 37
  • Captions, not checked by ear: the Trade Representative on Bloomberg Television, May 15, 2026, at 5:59 38
