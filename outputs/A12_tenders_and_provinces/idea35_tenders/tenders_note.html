China chip policy: purchase notices

What purchase notices name: Nvidia cards and home-made machines, 2024 to 2026

It is a sample found by search and proves nothing about proportions. 47 notices from Chinese universities, research institutes and one earthquake agency: 24 name Nvidia hardware, and 14 of those 24 are one university's (Wuhan University); 7 name or ask for home-made accelerator hardware (one of them, a Liaoning university's tender, asks only that the platform it buys support home-made chips it names, beside foreign ones); 16 name no accelerator maker or model. Later records add China Mobile's purchases, which by the trade press's accounts put the larger part, by value, of a 2025 purchase and all of a March 2026 tender on equipment of the CANN software ecosystem (Huawei's CANN, as the trade site says of the 2026 tender), and two universities' notices of July 2026 to buy Nvidia's RTX PRO 6000D. A notice to buy is neither a purchase made nor a delivery.

The question

How many published purchase notices of 2024 to 2026 name Nvidia hardware, and how many name home-made chips? A true census is not possible here. China's purchase notices sit on thousands of sites, many notices never name the chip, and the searches for the sample were made on one day. What follows is a bounded sample with a stated method and, apart from it, later records that later searches found.

What a purchase record can and cannot show

  • Can show. That a named buyer asked for, or chose, a named product, in a stated number, at a stated budget or price, on a stated date and under stated conditions, in its own words. A tender, purchase notice or purchase filing shows what was asked for. A result or award notice shows a contract decision; a result notice of a proposed award (拟成交) and a list of bid candidates (中标候选人) name the bidders proposed to win, before the award.
  • Cannot show. That the goods were delivered, paid for or put to use. How imported chips reached China, or whether a sale met United States export rules: no notice says. What share of all purchases went to Nvidia or to home-made hardware: many notices name no chip, and the notices here were found by search. Whose chips are in machines that a tender describes only by a software ecosystem (CUDA-like, CANN), unless the page says. And a trade site's account of a tender is not the tender itself.

Method

  • Start. Five Reuters reports that rest on tender documents and purchase records, and one university's own notice, were known before the sample. They are set out in the section on reports known before the sample.
  • Searches. 22 web searches on Nov 23, 2026, listed word for word in search_log.txt. Most were limited to the central government purchasing site (ccgp.gov.cn) or to universities' own sites (edu.cn). They asked for award notices (中标公告), result notices (成交公告) and purchase notices (采购公告) with Nvidia model names (H100, H200, H20, A100, A800, L40S, RTX 4090, RTX 5090) or with home-made names (昇腾 Ascend, Atlas, 寒武纪 Cambricon, 海光 Hygon, 昆仑芯 Kunlun, 鲲鹏 Kunpeng).
  • Saving. Results that looked like a purchase of GPU or AI hardware were saved: 69 addresses tried, 64 saved, 5 not saved (4 answered not found, 1 could not be reached). Not every result was opened: the choice was made from titles and summaries. No search form on any purchasing site was used.
  • Sorting. A notice goes in table 1 if it names Nvidia or an Nvidia model. It goes in table 2 if it names a home-made accelerator or a Huawei Atlas machine as the thing bought, asks for AI hardware under the word 国产 (home-made), as the Institute of Automation's tender does, or, as Liaoning's tender does, requires the platform bought to support home-made AI chips that it names, beside foreign ones. It goes in table 3 if the notice itself says GPU, AI, training or accelerated computing and names no accelerator by maker or model, even where it asks for GPU or NPU cards; most of these give a server brand and model. 17 saved pages are in no table: 6 whose notice does not itself say GPU, AI, training or accelerated computing, 3 dated before 2024, 3 that buy no accelerator, 3 that hold no usable content, and 2 that are not notices and are used further down. They are listed with reasons in left_out.txt.
  • Counting. Every count of the sample is computed by script from the table of notices (notices.txt) or from its saved pages, and every count of the later records from the table named beside it; none is typed. The year is the year of the date on the page; where the saved copy carries no date of its own, it is the year of the project number (Southern University of Science and Technology's and the Beijing Institute of Technology Zhuhai campus's notices) or of the purchase notice the award answers (Tsinghua University's Atlas award). The later records are not added to the counts: they were found another way.
  • Buyers. Each buyer is described only by what its name says (a university, a vocational college, a research institute, an agency). No saved page says whether a buyer is a public or state body, so the note does not say it.
  • From my general knowledge, not from the notices. Where a notice writes a model without Nvidia's name (L40, A6000, RTX 4090, RTX 4090 D, RTX 5090, H100), that these are Nvidia models is my knowledge; each such row says the maker is not written, except Nantong University's, whose notice names the card's board maker, 微星 (MSI, also my knowledge), and not Nvidia. That Huawei's Atlas machines run on Huawei's Ascend chips is also my knowledge; one notice writes the two names together 3.
