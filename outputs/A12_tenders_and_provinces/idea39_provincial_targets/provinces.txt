# Idea 39: what each province-level government's plan says on home-made computing hardware. One line per document; build.py reads this file.
# The mainland has 31 province-level governments: 22 provinces, 5 autonomous regions, 4 municipalities. Since round 4 the file covers all 31:
# the rows this folder read (rounds 1 to 3) and the rows found by later agents of the project (A13, L16, L25), whose table is
# ../../../tables/provinces_31.tsv. build.py stops if the groups here and in that table disagree.
# Fields, separated by " @@ ":
#   unit @@ group @@ document, issuing body and date, in English @@ source (a saved id) @@ the page's own words (";;" between several, "-" none)
#   @@ our English ("-" none) @@ remark ("-" none) @@ further strings checked in the page: title, number, date (";;" between several, "-" none;
#      an item that begins "[other-id] " is checked in that other saved copy)
#   @@ basis @@ where the page stands @@ label
# Group:  share     a share of home-made hardware is set (the nine: eight until core edit 8 of Nov 25, 2026, when Zhejiang's sector plan was found)
#         none      a plan was read and sets no share (20 governments)
#         account   the plan is known only from accounts of it, its issuer's own among them (Tibet)
#         notfound  no plan text found; another official page read in its place (Xinjiang)
#         other     a number that is not a share target (a reported rate, a reward, one project's mix)
#         also      another document read for a government of the share group; it sets no share
# Basis:  official  saved official page: a copy in the project's store of a page on the issuing body's own site or its government's own site
#         hand      saved by hand: a PDF of an official page that the owner saved in his browser
#         capture   saved Archive capture: a copy in the project's store of an Internet Archive capture of an official page
#                   ({CAP} in the where field is filled by build.py with the capture's date from its record)
#         copy      saved copy on another site: a copy in the project's store of the document's text on any other site
#         news      a newspaper's account: a saved page in which a newspaper describes the document; the document's own text is not held
# Label:  for rows of the share group, whether the page marks the share binding (约束性) or indicative (预期性); "-" for every other row.
#         build.py counts the two words in every page of the share group: only Jiangsu's holds them.
# Every string in the 5th and 8th fields is checked by build.py with scripts/cn_has.py.
# No straight double quotes in the rows of this file: the note's builder reads them as quotations.
