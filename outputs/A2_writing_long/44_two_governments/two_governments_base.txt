## Washington's steps

- **Dec 8, 2025. On the record.** The President posted that he had told Xi Jinping "the United States will allow NVIDIA to ship its H200 products to approved customers in China", and added: "President Xi responded positively!" [cn-clips-followup-3-relay1] That is the President's account, not Xi's words.
!! cn-clips-followup-3-relay1 | Truth Social Posts of December 8, 2025
- **Jan 15, 2026. On the record.** The Commerce Department's rule took effect. It moved licence applications for the H200 to China and Macau from a presumption of denial to case-by-case review, on conditions that include "independent, third-party testing in the United States" and shipments to China and Macau of at most half of the same product shipped for end use in the United States [man-ms-admin-bis-jan2026-rule-8ec187].

- **February 2026. On the record.** Nvidia's filing: from that month the US government "granted licenses that would allow us to ship small amounts of H200 products to specific China-based customers", with "a 25% tariff upon importation into the United States" [man-ms-sec-10q-2026-07].

## What the two lists show

Washington's record shows testing and volume limits. Nvidia's filing describes the tariff. The US table shows more chips in returned applications than in licensed ones. Reuters, citing people it did not name, reported first a hold and then conditional approvals. The Financial Times and Reuters cited unnamed people for their later reports on deliveries and chips in Hong Kong. Nvidia reported licensed sales of Hopper products to China.
What Beijing meant by it is not settled. Reuters wrote in January 2026 that it was unclear whether Beijing wanted an outright ban, "is still chewing over restrictions, or whether these measures could be used as a bargaining tactic in talks with Washington" [cn-acts-reuters-customs-20260114]. The US Trade Representative, May 15, 2026: whether Chinese companies may buy is "a sovereign decision for China" (captions, not checked by ear, at 5:59) [cn-le01-greer-bbg-20260515-subs-ttml]. The August delivery report covers two buyers. Reuters's report of Jan 28, 2026 covers three firms, and its report of Jan 30 adds a fourth [ms-bj-20260128-h200-nod-standard] [cn-ga31-sv1-reuters-deepseek-yahoo]. The record leaves open how many chips crossed into the mainland.
