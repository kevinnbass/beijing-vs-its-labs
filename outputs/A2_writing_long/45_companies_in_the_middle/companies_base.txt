## Between two governments

ByteDance, Alibaba, Tencent, Baidu and DeepSeek stood between two governments.
Since 2022 Washington has restricted which Nvidia chips may be exported to China [cn-clips-alibaba-20f].
!! cn-clips-alibaba-20f | since 2022, the U.S. government has implemented a series of measures restricting the export to China of advanced computing chips
From 2024 the planning side of their own state and the industry ministry, and from 2025 its security side, pressed China's AI companies, through unpublished guidance that the press reported, to use home-made chips instead.
Here is what the companies did, in five steps, in their own words where they spoke. Tencent's and Baidu's words come from a stock-data site's transcripts, whose speaker labels are unchecked.

**1. They used Nvidia chips, and were reported to order more**

A Shanghai government body wrote, in a reply dated Apr 30, 2025, that in the city internet companies' own computing clusters run mainly on Nvidia cards [cn-counter-b11-5].

**4. They reportedly trained abroad, and one lab was reported to go back to Nvidia for training**

The Financial Times, November 2025, by two people it cites: Alibaba and ByteDance "are among the tech groups training their latest large language models (LLMs) in data centres across Southeast Asia", leasing that is, by the paper's account, "compliant with US export controls" [cn-tolerate-ft-offshore].
!! cn-tolerate-ft-offshore | according to two people with direct knowledge of the matter
The same paper, Aug 13, 2025, by three unnamed people: DeepSeek "was encouraged by authorities to adopt Huawei's Ascend processor rather than use Nvidia's systems", met "persistent technical issues", and was prompted to use "Nvidia chips for training and Huawei's for inference", while it "was still working with Huawei to make the model compatible with Ascend for inference" [man-cn-ft-202508-deepseek].

**5. They built chips of their own and turned to other home-made chips**

Baidu's chief executive said in April 2025, in Reuters' words, that it had switched on a cluster "comprising 30,000 of its self-developed, third generation P800 Kunlun chips" [man-cn-labs-baidu-30k-reuters-alt1].
!! man-cn-labs-baidu-30k-reuters-alt1 | means switching the cluster on
Alibaba's chief executive, May 13, 2026, gave a commercial reason for its own chips: "gross margins on mainstream foreign AI chips are very high, basically in the 60% to 80% range", and "the high margins on advanced foreign chips leave domestic chips with enormous headroom for cost-performance gains" [cn-o36-alibaba-call-20260513-own].
!! cn-o36-alibaba-call-20260513-own | Wednesday, 13 May 2026
Tencent's chief strategy officer, Nov 18, 2026: "that inference can be run very effectively on a surprisingly limited number of domestic chips" [cn-o36-tencent-call-20261118-sa].
!! cn-o36-tencent-call-20261118-sa | Earnings Call: Q3 2026 Nov 18, 2026
Of DeepSeek's later model, V4, Reuters wrote in April 2026: "Huawei said its chips were used in some of the V4's training process" [cn-labs-v4-training-reuters-relay1-wayback]. Huawei Cloud's page of that day speaks of adapting and serving the model, and a post of that day signed Ascend CANN on the AtomGit open-source site, of a training reference implementation offered so that users can fine-tune quickly [cn-labs-v4-huawei-official-wayback] [cn-ga08-gitcode-ascend-v4-69eaff6d]; neither says that Huawei's chips trained V4. DeepSeek's own report on V4 names no chip for its training, and says one of its schemes was validated "on both NVIDIA GPUs and HUAWEI Ascend NPUs platforms" [cn-ga08-arxiv-deepseek-v4-pdf]. A rival, iFlytek, told investors days later that V4's training was still done on Nvidia cards, a competitor's claim that nobody has checked [cn-le26-iflytek-ir-20260429].

## What the five steps add up to

The companies were neither simply held back by their state nor simply in step with it.
The Financial Times put it in its own words in November 2025: training "requires a huge amount of computing power to process massive data sets, leading most Chinese groups to prefer Nvidia's advanced products for the task", while for inference, the everyday work of running a trained model, these companies were "increasingly turning to locally made Chinese chips" [cn-tolerate-ft-offshore]. Some of those chips are their own (step 5).
!! cn-tolerate-ft-offshore | PUBLISHED: 2025-11-27
Both are in the record at once.
