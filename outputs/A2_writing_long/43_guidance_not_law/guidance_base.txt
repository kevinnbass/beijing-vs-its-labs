## Pressure, and no published rule found

From 2024 the planning side of the Chinese state and the industry ministry, and from 2025 its security side, pressed China's AI companies, through unpublished guidance that the press reported, to buy home-made chips rather than Nvidia's. In public, state media warned about Nvidia's chips, most of the warnings found dating from Jul 31 to Sept 24, 2025; nothing read ties those texts to the guidance. No published order was found in the 82 policy pages read. Five things in the record show how that works.

**1. The press reports call it guidance** (press reports citing unnamed people)

Bloomberg, Sept 27, 2024 (carried by The Business Times on Sept 28, Singapore time): regulators were discouraging purchases of Nvidia's H20 chip through what it called window guidance, "instructions without the force of law" [cn-factions-bloomberg-flexibility-202409-relay1].

Reuters's "China bans foreign AI chips from state-funded data centres, sources say", Nov 5, 2025, describes guidance to new data-centre projects that took state money, by two unnamed people, not a published rule; by its sources, it was unclear whether that guidance applied nationwide or only in certain provinces [ms-bj-20251105-statefunded-standard].

## What guidance did here

No published rule on buying Nvidia's H200 chip was found, and what the reports said of buying it changed within two weeks, from a hold to approvals with conditions. An unnamed person told Reuters on Jan 14, 2026 that officials' wording "is basically a ban for now, though this might change in the future should things evolve" [cn-acts-reuters-customs-20260114]. On Jan 28 Reuters reported approvals "with conditions" for three companies [ms-bj-20260128-h200-nod-standard]. Nvidia's chief executive said on Jan 29, at Taipei's airport, as Voice of America's Chinese service renders him: "我认为中国政府仍在决策中，我们尚未收到采购订单，我们也没有收到新闻报道所说的相关信息" (I think the Chinese government is still deciding; we have not yet received purchase orders, and we have not received the information the news reports speak of) [cn-ga31-counter-40].

!! cn-acts-reuters-customs-20260114 | PUBLISHED: 2026-01-14
!! cn-acts-reuters-customs-20260114 | The wording from the officials is so severe that it
!! ms-bj-20260128-h200-nod-standard | PUBLISHED: 2026-01-28
So the record does not support the word ban: no published ban was found, and what the reports describe was told to companies, not published. Nor does it support the word nothing: Nvidia told the US securities regulator on Nov 19, 2025 that the Chinese government "restricted customers from purchasing, importing, or using our data center products" [man-ms-sec-10q-2025-10].
