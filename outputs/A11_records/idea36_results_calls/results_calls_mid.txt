
## The second table: other companies, and other records

These {M_ROWS} rows are of three kinds: {M_SA_ROWS} from {M_SA_CALLS} results calls of other companies, as the same stock-data site transcribes them; {M_OWN_ROWS} from the companies' own filings, investor records and one company blog; and {M_PRESS_ROWS} from a press report of a Tencent executive. {M_ZH_ROWS} of the {M_ROWS} are in Chinese. iFlytek's investor records list who attended for the company, not who gave each answer, so no speaker is named for them. Each row says what kind of record it is.
