## What was looked for

Every remark on chip supply by executives of Alibaba, Tencent and Baidu on their quarterly results calls: Nvidia chips, export controls, home-made chips, their own chips, and import approvals. The period is the 13 quarters reported from November 2023 to November 2026. A second table adds calls of other Chinese AI companies, and the filed reports and investor records of iFlytek, SenseTime and Zhipu, taken from the project's tables of what the companies said.

## Where

- **Alibaba's own transcripts.** Alibaba posts an English transcript of each call on its investor site. 12 were saved, for the calls of Nov 16, 2023 to Aug 20, 2026. Where an executive spoke Chinese these transcripts mark the English "(Translation)": the translation is Alibaba's [cn-o36-alibaba-call-20251125-own].
- **A stock-data site.** For Tencent and Baidu, and for Alibaba's call of Nov 19, 2026, the transcripts are from the stock-data site stockanalysis.com: 13 Tencent calls, 13 Baidu calls and one Alibaba call. The site's text and speaker labels are its own. They were not checked against the recordings.
- **Not used.** Alibaba's investor site listed no transcript yet for the call of Nov 19, 2026 when its data file was read on Nov 23, 2026 [cn-o36-index-alibaba-ir-q2026]. Baidu's investor site refused a request for its robots file (the file in which a site says what automated readers may fetch), so the site was not read for transcripts. The address tried for Tencent's quarterly results page did not exist (it returned 404), so whether Tencent posts transcripts of its own was not established.
- **The project's two tables of what the companies said** (tables/labs_said.tsv and tables/labs_said_more.tsv). They hold rows from 61 call transcripts of eight companies and from other records: the filings and investor records of iFlytek, SenseTime and Zhipu, a Meituan blog and a press report among them. The second table here takes 20 of their rows by script. I checked each quotation against its saved copy and, for the call transcripts, the speaker label and the date on the page. I did not read those calls and documents whole.

**Coverage of the first table.** All 39 calls of the 13 quarters were saved: no quarter is missing. 30 calls yielded at least one row. In the other 9 the reading found no remark on chip supply beyond spending on GPUs and renting them out: Alibaba (Feb 7, 2024, May 14, 2024, Aug 15, 2024, Nov 15, 2024); Baidu (Feb 18, 2025); Tencent (Mar 20, 2024, May 14, 2024, Aug 14, 2024, Nov 13, 2024).

**How the rows were chosen.** A script listed every paragraph of the 39 transcripts that holds one of a list of words (chip, GPU, Nvidia, export, import, semiconductor, domestic, self-developed and others): 374 paragraphs. I read the sentences that hold those words, with the sentence before and after each, and kept the remarks on supply. Remarks only about spending or about renting GPUs to customers were left out. The speaker and the date of each row are read from the page by the build script.
