## What was looked for

Every remark on chip supply by executives of Alibaba, Tencent and Baidu on their quarterly results calls: Nvidia chips, export controls, home-made chips, their own chips, and import approvals. The period is the {N_Q} quarters reported from November 2023 to November 2026. A second table adds calls of other Chinese AI companies, and the filed reports and investor records of iFlytek, SenseTime and Zhipu, from rows that two other readers of the project found.

## Where

- **Alibaba's own transcripts.** Alibaba posts an English transcript of each call on its investor site. {N_OWN} were saved, for the calls of {OWN_FIRST} to {OWN_LAST}. Where an executive spoke Chinese these transcripts mark the English "(Translation)": the translation is Alibaba's [cn-o36-alibaba-call-20251125-own].
- **A stock-data site.** For Tencent and Baidu, and for Alibaba's call of Nov 19, 2026, the transcripts are from the stock-data site stockanalysis.com: {N_SA_TENCENT} Tencent calls, {N_SA_BAIDU} Baidu calls and {N_SA_ALIBABA} Alibaba call. The site's text and speaker labels are its own. They were not checked against the recordings.
- **Not used.** Alibaba's investor site listed no transcript yet for the call of Nov 19, 2026 when its data file was read on Nov 23, 2026 [cn-o36-index-alibaba-ir-q2026]. Baidu's investor site refused a request for its robots file (the file in which a site says what automated readers may fetch), so the site was not read for transcripts. The address tried for Tencent's quarterly results page did not exist (it returned 404), so whether Tencent posts transcripts of its own was not established.
- **The project's two tables of what the companies said** (tables/labs_said.tsv and tables/labs_said_more.tsv). Two other readers of the project made them, from {L9_CALLS} call transcripts of eight companies and from iFlytek's and SenseTime's own documents. The second table here takes {M_ROWS} of their rows by script. I checked each quotation against its saved copy and, for the call transcripts, the speaker label and the date on the page. I did not read those calls and documents whole.

**Coverage of the first table.** All {N_CALLS} calls of the {N_Q} quarters were saved: no quarter is missing. {N_CALLS_ROWS} calls yielded at least one row. In the other {N_CALLS_NOROWS} the reading found no remark on chip supply beyond spending on GPUs and renting them out: {NOROW_LIST}.

**How the rows were chosen.** A script listed every paragraph of the {N_CALLS} transcripts that holds one of a list of words (chip, GPU, Nvidia, export, import, semiconductor, domestic, self-developed and others): {N_CAND} paragraphs. I read the sentences that hold those words, with the sentence before and after each, and kept the remarks on supply. Remarks only about spending or about renting GPUs to customers were left out. The speaker and the date of each row are read from the page by the build script.
