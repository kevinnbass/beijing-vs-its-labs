## Quote the calls, not the commission's appendix

The US-China commission's report of November 2026 prints two of the Tencent remarks above in its Appendix II [cn-usdocs-uscc-2026-ch8]. Its notes give Chinese transcripts of the two calls on Sina Technology as the source. So the appendix's English is the commission's own rendering of those Chinese transcripts. The stock-data site's transcripts of the two calls are in English, and a script finds no mark of an untranslated passage in either; neither fact shows how the site made its text, or in what language each answer was first given. Their text and speaker labels are the site's, unchecked against the recordings, and who spoke on the May call is not settled (see finding 6).
