What was looked for

Every remark on chip supply by executives of Alibaba, Tencent and Baidu on their quarterly results calls: Nvidia chips, export controls, home-made chips, their own chips, and import approvals. The period is the 13 quarters reported from November 2023 to November 2026. A second table adds calls of other Chinese AI companies, and the filed reports and investor records of iFlytek, SenseTime and Zhipu, taken from the project's tables of what the companies said.

How the rows were chosen. A script listed every paragraph of the 39 transcripts that holds one of a list of words (chip, GPU, Nvidia, export, import, semiconductor, domestic, self-developed and others): 374 paragraphs. I read the sentences that hold those words, with the sentence before and after each, and kept the remarks on supply. Remarks only about spending or about renting GPUs to customers were left out. The speaker and the date of each row are read from the page by the build script.

22024 and 2025, in the companies' own words on their calls: a “stockpile” at Tencent, scarce imported GPUs, and mixed clusters. 3August 2025: on imports of US chips, no answer and two governments. Tencent's president: “we don't really have a definite answer on the import situation yet. I think there's a lot of discussion between the two governments, and we're waiting to see what exactly comes out of that.” 15 He names no Chinese body and no order. A Chinese site, DoNews, in an item dated Aug 18, 2025 that cites foreign media, drew a further conclusion and marked it as its own reading: “从刘炽平的表态来看，即便目前英伟达H20和AMD MI308已经恢复了对华出口，但是腾讯目前并不会继续采购。” (judging from Lau's remarks, even though Nvidia's H20 and AMD's MI308 have now resumed export to China, Tencent will not for now go on buying) 49. The transcript holds no such statement by him, and a script's count finds no H20 in it. So a line that Tencent had no plan to buy the H20 is an outlet's reading, not his words.

4November 2025: Tencent lowered its spending plan for want of chips. Asked whether the lower plan reflected chip availability, its president said: “It is indeed a change in terms of the AI chip availability.” 18

5March 2026: imported GPUs “available again”. Tencent's chief strategy officer said new capacity would come from leasing, from “higher-end imported GPUs which are now becoming available again”, and from China-designed GPUs 21. On the same call its president said designing chips of its own was not the priority: “you actually want to have access to the most state-of-the-art, you know, training chips to the extent possible” 21. Quote the calls, not the commission's appendix

The US-China commission's report of November 2026 prints two of the Tencent remarks above in its Appendix II 54. Its notes give Chinese transcripts of the two calls on Sina Technology as the source. The stock-data site's transcripts of the two calls are in English, and a script finds no mark of an untranslated passage in either; neither fact shows how the site made its text, or in what language each answer was first given.   • March 2026, Tencent's president. The appendix: “If conditions allow us to purchase” 54. The call: “we would actually prefer to buy because then we don't necessarily need to pay the additional margin for leasing” 21. The sense is the same.
  • May 2026, a speaker on its call (the chief strategy officer by one transcript's label, the chief financial officer by another's). The appendix has policy restrictions that “prevent some foreign-produced and” foreign-designed GPUs from entering the Chinese market 54. The call: “a combination of policy restrictions on certain, you know, foreign design GPUs being brought into China” 24.
