## What was looked for

The committee's own page for the hearing at which the Commerce Secretary said the Chinese central government had not let its companies buy the chips; the committee's video; and any official transcript or printed record. The argument cites a commercial transcript (by the firm Rev) for his words. The aim was an official source to stand beside it.

## What else was said: the same day, and at two other hearings

Another of the project's readers went through the whole caption track of this hearing and through two House hearings of 2026, with search words and controls. I checked that the words quoted below stand in the saved transcripts; I did not read those hearings whole.

1. **Apr 22, 2026, the same hearing, earlier.** To Senator Shaheen the Secretary said: "I want to be crystal clear, we are not selling our best chips to China under any circumstance." And of China and the chips: "I would tell you they have not bought any as of today." [cn-us-lutnick-0422-rev-transcript-b] He gave no reason in that answer. By that reader's listing of the committee's captions it comes at about 0:52:30, some 25 minutes before the exchange with Senator Coons. Only the answer to Senator Coons gives a reason, and he gave it as his understanding: "So that's my understanding of what's happening." [cn-us-lutnick-0422-rev-transcript-b]
2. **Feb 24, 2026, a House Foreign Affairs subcommittee** ("Strengthening Export Control Enforcement", "Tuesday, February 24, 2026" [cn-le02-house-118974-sd001]). A member asked David Peters, the Commerce Department's assistant secretary for export enforcement: "Do you know how many H200s have been sold to Chinese end users so far?" He answered: "Uh my understanding is that so far none" [ms-g17-x3-firstbranch-22a30d]. He gave no reason.
!! ms-g17-x3-firstbranch-22a30d | Assistant Secretary David Peters, BIS Export Enforcement
3. **Jul 14, 2026, the House Foreign Affairs Committee** ("FY27 BIS Budget: the AI Arms Race and the ICTS Office", "Tuesday, July 14, 2026" [cn-le02-house-119444-sd001]). Asked whether H200 shipments had begun, Jeffrey Kessler, the under secretary of commerce for industry and security, said: "To my knowledge, uh, yes, but very few." And later: "there have been minimal, um, exports of any H two hundreds to China so far" [ms-g17-x3-hearinghighl-979923]. He named no cause, Chinese or American.
!! ms-g17-x3-hearinghighl-979923 | Jeffrey Kessler (Under Secretary of Commerce for Industry and Security, U.S. Department of Commerce)
4. **No answer at either House hearing speaks of Beijing's part.** That reader searched YouTube's automatic captions of each committee's video and the saved commercial transcript for words such as Chinese government, central government, Beijing, allow, approve, block, refuse, customs and import. It found no answer by either official that says the Chinese government approved, blocked, discouraged or held up H200 purchases. The search could have found one. The same words are found where members used them on other matters: "the Chinese government released new standards" on Jul 14 [ms-g17-x3-hearinghighl-979923], and "PRC government's approval" on Feb 24 [ms-g17-x3-firstbranch-22a30d].
5. **Neither House hearing's page links a print or a transcript.** The House's page for each lists under its hearing record only the members who attended: "Hearing Record Member Attendance" [cn-le02-house-event-119444] [ms-g17-lake-hearing-118974]. The two transcripts quoted here are commercial ones, with the sites' own speaker labels.

So of the three hearings, only the answer to Senator Coons on Apr 22, 2026 says anything about the Chinese government and the purchases, and it is the Secretary's understanding, not a finding.

## Where the argument stands on this hearing

- **The source of the words, and the date.** The argument's point {P12} cites the committee's page for the hearing and its date, and takes the Secretary's words from the commercial transcript, since the committee has posted no transcript of the exchange.
- **The captions.** The argument's list of open points says that a search found the same sentence in the committee video's own captions, at {ARG_CAP_FROM} to {ARG_CAP_TO}, a reading with no saved copy. The {N_VTT} caption files kept beside this note put the start of the answer at {C_BEGIN}, the key sentence at {C_KEY} and the end of the answer at {C_END}.
- **The quoted words stand.** Both sentences the argument quotes are in the speech program's hearing of the official sound (checked by script).
- **Quote the commercial transcript from its saved page, not from its text file.** The text file has lost the Senator's name, as set out above; the saved page keeps the speaker labels.
- **No print yet.** No print was found in the places looked. The fiscal 2025 volume's hearings were held from April to June 2024, and the volume was printed in 2025; that gives no date for a print of this hearing. The official record for now is the video.
- **The other hearings add no second voice.** The officials at the hearings of Feb 24 and Jul 14, 2026 said how few H200s had been sold or shipped, and nothing about why. They neither bear out the Secretary's account nor contradict it.
- **His own understanding.** The Secretary gave his statement as his own understanding, as he says more than once.
