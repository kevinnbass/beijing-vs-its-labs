## How the two clips fit together

Pottinger blames Jensen Huang for China's AI models. Luckey says that "China doesn't want NVIDIA to sell GPUs to Chinese companies" (sentence L3) [1]. The two need not clash, and the record bears out only a narrower part of each: that Chinese companies used Nvidia chips, not who is to blame for their models; and that from 2024 Beijing pressed its companies to move away from Nvidia chips, not that it kept them all out. Chinese companies trained models on Nvidia chips: DeepSeek by its own technical report (sentence P4), Alibaba and ByteDance abroad by one press report (sentence P12). By Washington's own table, none of the Nvidia chips in the approved applications it lists, at their status on May 11, 2026, had shipped (sentence P12). On the H200 chips at issue in 2026, Nvidia's report for the quarter to Jul 26, 2026 says shipments under the H200 licences "account for less than 1% of Data Center revenue in our most recent quarter" [21]. On its results call for the quarter ended Oct 25, 2026 it said: "Q3 sales of licensed Hopper Data Center products to China was $2.4 billion" (Reuters, reporting the call: "The Hopper family includes the H200" [45]), and then "we are no longer shipping licensed Hopper Data Center products to China" [46]. It gave no reason, and it did not say how many of those chips crossed into mainland China.
