## The same point, said so that it holds

From 2024 the security and planning side of the Chinese state pressed China's AI companies, through unpublished guidance that the press reported, to buy home-made chips rather than Nvidia's, and from the summer of 2025 to stop ordering them, while the security ministry warned in public of backdoors in chips made abroad and state media warned about Nvidia's chips, most of all in August and September 2025; other parts of the state welcomed Nvidia and approved some purchases; the companies went on ordering Nvidia chips in the millions and, by the reports, had received a small part of them by late 2026; they were also turning to home-made chips, for reasons of their own and under American curbs; and whether Beijing meant its acts as lasting policy or as leverage in trade talks is read both ways.
