# The record as a dated list
@deck 62 dated records of what Chinese authorities did about Nvidia chips and what China's AI companies did, as plain lines for pasting.

@note Draft by Claude, Anthropic's model.
@note Made by a script (gen_timeline.py) from the figure's table of records, figure/figure_events.tsv. Nothing was retyped. 62 records, in date order, from May 21, 2023 to Nov 19, 2026.
@note Each line: date | who | what | source | address. The second field, who, is the row of the figure the record sits in: which side it is about and which way it points. It is not always the speaker.
@note Two tiers, marked at the end of each source: 36 records are on the record (22 published documents or officials, 14 companies or named people outside government); 26 are press reports citing unnamed people.
@note By row: Beijing holds Nvidia back, 24; Beijing lets Nvidia in, 14; The companies reach for Nvidia, 14; The companies turn to home-made chips, 10.
@note Words between quotation marks are the source's own: all 30 such phrases were found in the saved copy of the record's source by the script.
@note English given for Chinese sources is ours, not an official translation.
@note 2 records have no exact day, and their date field carries the figure's own words: After summer 2025 (Wall Street Journal); Sept 2025 (Alibaba, Form 20-F filed with the SEC). Each stands in the list where the figure places it; that place is not a date.
