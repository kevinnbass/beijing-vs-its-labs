## The posts

**1.** Palmer Luckey, clip posted Nov 19, 2026 (0:11, machine transcript): "China doesn't want NVIDIA to sell GPUs to Chinese companies." Then the post's text: "there are Chinese companies that want to buy Nvidia GPUs". Not checked by ear. Right on direction, with five limits. [1] [2]

**2.** The declared line: use home-made chips. At its press conference of May 22, 2026, Li Chao of China's state planner (NDRC) said it was guiding home-made large models to do more to adapt to home-made computing chips. Source: the planner's own record. English ours. [3]

**3.** What the companies used: Nvidia. A Shanghai government body wrote (reply dated Apr 30, 2025; published Nov 10, 2025) that in the city internet firms' own clusters run mainly on Nvidia cards, while the three big telecoms mainly buy Huawei's and other home-made clusters. [4]

**4.** What Beijing did. Alibaba's annual report to the US SEC: "In September 2025, the Cyberspace Administration of China requested Chinese technology companies to halt purchases of certain chips." The sentence does not name Nvidia. This is a filing, not an unnamed source. [5]

**5.** Limit 1: Beijing was not one actor. On Jul 17, 2025 China's commerce minister told Nvidia's chief executive he hoped multinationals, Nvidia among them, would give Chinese customers high-quality, reliable products and services. Source: the ministry's account; English ours. [6]

**6.** Limit 2: warnings and guidance, not a published ban. Bloomberg, Sept 27, 2024, citing unnamed people: "The policy has taken the form of guidance rather than an outright ban". In 82 Chinese policy pages read, no published order against buying Nvidia chips was found. [7]

**7.** Limit 3: Reuters, citing people it did not name, reported orders for over 2 million H200s for 2026 and, separately, conditional approvals for over 400,000 H200s. Nvidia's filings report licensed sales to China, including about $60 million in H20 revenue. [8] [9] [10]

**8.** Limit 4: the companies had reasons of their own, and US export curbs pushed too. Tencent's president, on a results call in November 2023, after new US curbs: "we will also try to look for domestic source for these training chips" (as Reuters reported the call). [11]

**9.** Limit 5: what Beijing meant is unsettled. A US commission's report to Congress read the market regulator's Nvidia move "as a negotiating tactic". The US Trade Representative, asked May 15, 2026 if China would let firms buy H200s: "a sovereign decision for China" (captions). [12] [13]

**10.** From 2024 China's planners and industry ministry, and from 2025 its security side, pushed firms toward home-made chips in guidance the press reported. Published local targets also favored them. Firms kept seeking Nvidia and buying home-made chips. Beijing's intent is open.
