## How to use this page

This page is for a reader who wants to check our English against the Chinese. Each row gives one passage: who issued it and when, the page's own words, and our English. The number after the source leads to the reference list, which gives the page's address and the name of the saved copy.

The 48 passages are taken from the argument (ARGUMENT_referenced.md): what it quotes from Chinese state bodies, the Party, state media and a few companies. By a script's count they hold 52 of the argument's 86 Chinese quotations, some at greater length; the 34 left out are mostly from company filings, the trade press and purchase notices. The English is the argument's own wording wherever it gives one. A second reader went through the argument's Chinese quotations on Nov 23, 2026 and found faults in the English beside 34 of the 50 it then had; the argument was corrected, and this page takes the corrected wording. The second reader is not a native reader of Chinese, and no native reader has checked this page.

Most passages are texts that a Chinese state body or the Party published, or an official's words on an official site. Some are not: state-media commentaries and an industry body's statement (section 3), company filings and two company chiefs' words as newspapers reported them (section 4), a company's question on a ministry's message board (passage 36), and a scholar's advice printed by a Party paper (passage 42). Each row says which. None is a press report from unnamed people: Beijing's instructions to companies are reported that way and are not on this page.

A passage that is found shows what the page says. It does not show that the words were carried out.
