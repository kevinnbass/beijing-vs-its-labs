## How to read the rows

The question behind this page: did Beijing's chip policy pull against what China's own AI companies wanted? The answer of the argument (ARGUMENT_referenced.md) is yes on direction, with five limits. One limit is that Beijing was not one actor; another is that what Beijing meant by its acts is not settled. This page shows who the actors were.

Two kinds of statement stand in the last column and are kept apart. **On the record** means a published document, a filing, or a named person's words. **Reported** means a press report that cites unnamed people. What Beijing told companies in private, which Chinese bodies approved what, and what China's customs did rest on press reports, not on published policy. Two companies' filings say in general words what Beijing did, and their rows below give them: Nvidia's, that the Chinese government restricted customers from buying its data-centre products, and Alibaba's, that the cyberspace regulator in September 2025 asked companies to halt purchases of certain chips, which it does not name.
