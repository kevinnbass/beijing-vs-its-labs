China · Nvidia chips · six sentences from the record

Beijing mostly pulled one way on Nvidia chips. Its AI companies pulled the other.

Three sentences show the Chinese state pulling away from Nvidia. Three show China’s AI companies pulling toward it. Three are on the record; three are press reports citing unnamed people.

  • Filled mark: on the record (a government document, a filing, a named official)
  • Open mark: a press report citing unnamed people

Beijing pulls away from Nvidia

“Authorities privately told companies to halt their Nvidia purchases, while officials publicly raised cybersecurity concerns about the U.S. company’s products.”

The Wall Street Journal, Jul 23, 2026, on the summer of 2025A press report citing unnamed people.

“In September 2025, the Cyberspace Administration of China requested Chinese technology companies to halt purchases of certain chips.”

Alibaba, annual report to the US securities regulator (Form 20-F), for the year to Mar 31, 2026On the record: a company filing. The sentence does not name Nvidia.

“指导国产大模型加大力度适配国产算力芯片”

(guiding home-made large models to do more to adapt to home-made computing chips)

Li Chao, of the state planner’s policy research office (NDRC), press conference of May 22, 2026On the record: a named official. The sentence does not name Nvidia.

The companies pull toward it

“Chinese firms still want Nvidia chips despite government pressure not to buy, sources say”

Reuters, headline of Sept 4, 2025A press report citing unnamed people.

“互联网企业自建算力集群，满足市场化企业需求，主要以英伟达高性能卡为主”

(internet companies’ own computing clusters, which serve market demand, run mainly on Nvidia high-performance cards)

Shanghai’s Commission of Economy and Informatization, written reply dated Apr 30, 2025 (published Nov 10, 2025)On the record: a government document. It speaks of computing in Shanghai.

“Chinese technology companies have placed orders for more than 2 million H200 chips for 2026”

Reuters, Dec 31, 2025A press report citing unnamed people.

The limit

“The policy has taken the form of guidance rather than an outright ban”

Bloomberg, Sept 27, 2024, as carried by The Business Times on Sept 28, 2024A press report citing unnamed people.

By this and later reports the pressure came as unpublished guidance; state media also warned in public, in the 7 items found that only warn about Nvidia’s chips, all dated Aug 1 to Sept 24, 2025. No published Chinese order against buying Nvidia chips was found in the 103 policy pages read. This is one of five limits on the headline. The others: Beijing was not one actor; some purchases were approved and some chips came in; the companies had reasons of their own, and American curbs pushed too; and what Beijing meant by it is read both ways.
