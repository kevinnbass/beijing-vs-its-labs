The evidence and the sources

  • The official record: government documents and company filings, each with the source's own words.
  • The reporting: press reports, each marked checked, relayed or a lead.
  • The tables: the counts behind the argument and the figure as 21 plain tables (the licence table, Nvidia's wording by filing, the 31 provinces, the local vouchers, the state-media items and others).
  • Every source: 1114 saved pages with address, time saved and hash. The text of press pages is not included; the text of 24 government documents is.
  • The scripts that looked for each quotation in its saved copy and built the pages.
  • The repository's own description, what it leaves out and why, and the licence.

How these were checked

  • Every quotation was looked for in a saved copy of its source by a script; a build stops if one is missing. A quotation that is found shows what the source says, not that it is true.
  • Sources are in two tiers: documents, filings and named people on the record; and press reports citing unnamed people, a research firm's counts, and captions or machine transcripts nobody has checked by ear.
  • Every page and image was rendered and looked at before being called done.
  • Not done: nobody has listened to the clips or watched the two videos play, and no native reader has reviewed the Chinese figure.
