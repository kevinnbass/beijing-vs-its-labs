# What this repository leaves out, and why

First made public Nov 23, 2026; this version assembled Nov 28, 2026 (UTC). The files below are not in this repository: once public they cannot be taken back, and they are other people's work, the text of saved articles, or working notes not written for readers. Each kind can be added later.

- Other people's video and sound: the two interview clips, the captioned video made from one of them (item 23 of the list of outputs), the listening sheet that plays them (item 40), sound cuts and stills. 45 files.
- Working notes: the running record, the checklist of open leads, each builder's notes of what it changed and the working tools it set apart, logs, and copies of files kept from before an edit. 699 files.
- Scratch, archive and cache folders, earlier drafts and intermediate render files. 4453 files.
- Paragraphs of a commercial transcript site, kept as working files of the results-calls note. 3 files.
- The builders' scratch tools of the reference pages: trial files, their outputs and earlier copies. 21 files.
- One saved page's address and record in the list of sources, withheld pending a decision on publishing them.
- The project's file of the two clips' machine transcripts, which some pages here name as held back: a speech program's words, not checked by ear; the argument quotes short passages of one of them. 1 file.

## Of the series of images

The 34 images are in `series/` as their public edition was built: each image as drawn and looked at, its parts naming the documents behind them with their public addresses, and only the words the image itself prints. Nothing else of the series' working folder is here:

- The local viewer's reader pages: they show the text of saved articles round each passage an image rests on. 8678 files.
- The scripts that build the series: their own checks hold, word for word, the names and phrases the images must not print, so publishing them would print what the images leave out. 40 scripts (and 370 earlier copies of them).
- The series' working notes and data tables: instructions to the builders, decisions on wording, notes of each stage of revision, records of the edits to the argument and the figure, records of each check of a page by eye, and the tables the figures are drawn from. 14798 files.
- The figure pages and pictures the series is drawn from, and the local atlas pages that link them to the reader pages. 240 files.
- Earlier builds of the public edition. 3420 files.

The saved copies of press articles were never part of this repository: `sources.csv` gives each one's address and hash.

Changes made to the copied files. In the copies under `outputs/`: this machine's folder names made relative (66 text files); and the key that Nvidia's investor site puts in its own feed addresses written as SITE_PUBLIC_KEY (17 addresses). In the text files outside `series/`, where they stood: one phrase on copies that the project itself no longer cites reads "since withdrawn" or "since replaced" (1 text file under `outputs/` and 1 text file elsewhere in the repository); and lines that called a page local or not published, and pointers to files that are held back, reworded (3 text files under `outputs/` and 1 text file elsewhere in the repository).
