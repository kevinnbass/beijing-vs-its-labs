# What this repository leaves out, and why

First made public Nov 23, 2026; this version assembled Nov 26, 2026 (UTC). The files below are not in this repository: once public they cannot be taken back, and they are other people's work, the text of saved articles, or working notes not written for readers. Each kind can be added later.

- Other people's video and sound: the two interview clips, the captioned video made from one of them (item 23 of the list of outputs), the listening sheet that plays them (item 40), sound cuts and stills. 45 files.
- Working notes: the running record, the checklist of open leads, each builder's notes of what it changed, logs and before-copies. 439 files.
- Scratch, archive and cache folders, earlier rounds and intermediate render files. 2163 files.
- Paragraphs of a commercial transcript site, kept as working files of the results-calls note. 3 files.
- The builders' scratch tools of the reference pages: trial files, their outputs and earlier copies. 20 files.
- One saved page's address and record in the list of sources, withheld pending a decision on publishing them.

## Of the series of images

The 34 images are in `series/` as their public edition was built: each image as drawn and looked at, its parts naming the documents behind them with their public addresses, and only the words the image itself prints. Nothing else of the series' working folder is here:

- The local viewer's reader pages: they show the text of saved articles round each passage an image rests on. 6587 files.
- The scripts that build the series: their own checks name, word for word, what the project leaves out. 38 scripts (and 235 earlier copies of them).
- The series' working notes and data tables: briefs, rulings, round notes, records of the edits to the argument and the figure, records of each look at a page, and the tables the figures are drawn from. 2228 files.
- The figure pages and pictures the series is drawn from, and the local atlas pages that link them to the reader pages. 240 files.
- Earlier builds of the public edition. 2394 files.

The saved copies of press articles were never part of this repository: `sources.csv` gives each one's address and hash.

Changes made to the copied files. In the copies under `outputs/`: this machine's folder names made relative (67 text files); and the key that Nvidia's investor site puts in its own feed addresses written as SITE_PUBLIC_KEY (17 addresses).
